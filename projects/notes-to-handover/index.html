Everyday AI · Organise the loose ends

A short handover is easy to postpone. The useful AI step is separating what was agreed from what someone merely suggested.

What to check


Check every action against its source phrase. Confirm that suggestions remain tentative and that missing owners and dates stay visible.


This exercise demonstrates a useful way to ask for help, not a guarantee of model accuracy or measured time savings. The examples are invented. A person decides which findings are valid and what to do next.












TipReusable instructions







Turn the notes into a short handover with Decisions, Actions, and Open questions. For each action show the owner and deadline only when explicitly stated; otherwise write Unassigned or Not stated. Preserve tentative wording, dependencies and unresolved questions. Quote the source phrase supporting each action. Do not turn a suggestion into a commitment.







Read the first worked example without running the demo

The first example’s input appears above. Its illustrative response is:


DECISION
Keep the current intake form for this month.

ACTIONS
• Send the revised checklist — Sam; Thursday (calendar date not stated). Source: “Sam will send the revised checklist by Thursday.”
• Confirm the shared mailbox owner — Unassigned; deadline not stated. Source: “Need to check who owns the shared mailbox.”
• Finish the report once the export arrives — Unassigned; deadline not stated. Source: “report can only be finished after it arrives.”

OPEN QUESTIONS
• Can Priya cover next week? This is a suggestion, not an agreed assignment.
• Who will supply the missing export, and when?
